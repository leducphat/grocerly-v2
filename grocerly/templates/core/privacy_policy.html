{% trans "Privacy Policy" %}

{% trans "Last updated: 5 November 2026" %}

{% trans "Grocerly is an online grocery shop built as a graduation thesis at HCMC University of Technology and Engineering. This page explains what the shop keeps about you, why, and who else sees it." %}

{% trans "What we keep" %}

  • {% trans "Your account: email address, user name and password. The password is stored only as a hash, never as readable text." %}
  • {% trans "Your profile, if you fill it in: full name, phone number, address, a short bio and a photo." %}
  • {% trans "Your orders: the name, email, phone number and delivery address you enter at checkout, the products, the prices and whether the order is paid." %}
  • {% trans "The reviews you write, the products you keep in your wishlist, and the messages you send through the contact form." %}

{% trans "Payment" %}

{% trans "Online payments go through VNPay. You type your card or bank details on VNPay's page, not on Grocerly, so the shop never sees or stores them. Grocerly only keeps what VNPay sends back: whether the payment succeeded." %}

{% trans "The AI assistant" %}

{% trans "What you type in the chat window is sent to Google's Gemini service, which writes the reply. Please do not type personal details such as your phone number or address into the chat." %}

{% trans "Emails" %}

{% trans "When you ask to reset your password, the email is sent through the Brevo email service. Grocerly does not send newsletters or advertising." %}

{% trans "Cookies" %}

{% trans "The shop uses only the cookies it needs to work: one keeps you signed in and holds your cart, one remembers the language you chose, and one protects the forms against requests from other sites. There are no advertising or tracking cookies." %}

{% trans "Who sees your data" %}

{% trans "Only the shop staff, to handle your orders and messages. Your data is not sold or given to anyone else, apart from the services named on this page that the shop needs in order to work." %}

{% trans "Your choices" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %}You can change your profile at any time from your account. To have your account deleted, or to ask about this policy, write to us through the contact page.{% endblocktrans %}
